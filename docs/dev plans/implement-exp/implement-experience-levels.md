## Plan and implement experience system and upgrade mechanic, to replace the in game store. 

Each time an enemy dies, the player will receive an amount of experience proportional to the enemies infestation value. The player will accumulate experience until they collect enough experience to reach the next level, at which point the game pauses and the player is allowed to choose from three random upgrades options. The player will be able to see their experience and level number in a bar across the top of the arena. It will be a long thin silver panel pinned to the top of the arena, and 80% of the width of the bar will be a thinner bar that fills up left to right, where the left end is a small bulb. When the player has no experience, the experience bar is black.  As the player gains experience, the bar fills up with a bright saturated neon blue.  The experience bar will visually reflect the percentage of experience until the next level is reached. To the right of the experience bar is the player's current level number in thick bold text. the first level will cost 12 experience.  Each level requires 3% more experience than the last.

The upgrade options will be presented as three rectangular panels, taller than wide, displayed left to right. The paused game behind it will be dimmed. Once the player chooses an upgrade, the upgrade options will disappear, the necessary trap or boost stats will be updated, and the game will unpause. The upgrade panels should have color themes based on tier. There will be three tiers: Common (low), Professional (medium), and Rare (high). The higher the tier, the higher the reward.  Common tier will be green, professional will be blue, and rare will be purple.

the three upgrade options presented to the player will be randomly selected from the following groups of upgrade types
- equipment level rewards, Every stat that can be upgraded in the upgrade panel
- campaign level rewards, global buffs like +<bonus percent> that increases damage or range for all traps / boosts, present me some options about other useful items in this category

tier of each upgrade upgrade option presented will be determined randomly based on a graduated scale:
- there is a 1% chance that an upgrade option is rare
- there is a 15% chance that an upgrade option is professional
- otherwise the upgrade option will be common
