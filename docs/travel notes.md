## Plan and implement experience system and upgrade mechanic, to replace the in game store. 

Each time an enemy dies, the player will receive an amount of experience proportional to the enemies infestation value. The player will accumulate experience until the current level is maxed out, at which point the game pauses and the player is allowed to choose from three random upgrades options. Each level requires about 5% more experience than the last. The player will be able to see their experience and level in a bar across the top of the arena. It will be a long run panel pinned to the top of the arena, and 80% of the width of the bar will be a thinner blue bar that fills up left to right, where the left end is a small bulb. The experience bar will visually reflect the percentage of experience until the next level is reached. To the right of the experience bar is the player's current level number in thick bold text.

The upgrade options will be presented as three rectangular panels, taller than wide, displayed left to right. The pauses game behind it will be dimme. Once the player chooses an upgrade, the upgrade options will disappear, the necessary trap or boost stats will be updated, and the game will unpause. The upgrade panels should have color themes based on tier. There will be three tiers: Common, Professional, and Rare. The higher the tier, the higher the reward.  Common tier will be green, professional will be blue, and rare will be purple.
